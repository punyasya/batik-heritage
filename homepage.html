Our Heritage

Preserving the Royal Legacy of Surakarta

Founded in the heart of Solo, Benang Ratu Heritage is dedicated to the preservation and elevation of Batik Tulis — the world's most sophisticated textile art. Every garment we create is a bridge between the regal history of the keraton and the modern silhouette of the world citizen.

We don't just make clothing; we archive stories in silk and cotton, ensuring the wisdom of our ancestors continues to inspire the future.
